Pocket Chess Plus – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Pocket Chess Plus (package com.pocketapps.pocket_chess), published by Digital Dignitas ("we"), handles information.

Summary

We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. The app works fully offline.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • the stars you earned on each level,
  • your Shards balance (the in-game currency used for hints),
  • the date you last claimed the daily gift.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it.

Network access, analytics and advertising

The app does not request internet access, does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, does not show ads and does not use the advertising ID. It contains no account system and no sign-in.

Permissions

The app does not request any runtime permissions (such as location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no personal data, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle data differently (for example advertising), we will update this page and the effective date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: dizitalniche@gmail.com
