Ads and rewards

The app is free and shows ads (banner, full-screen, app-open and optional rewarded videos). Some rewards, such as the daily gift or extra hints, are unlocked by watching a rewarded video. Whether a video is available depends on the ad network, and a reward is only given when the video is completed.

In-app purchases

  • Purchases are made through Google Play at the price shown before you buy.
